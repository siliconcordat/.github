## 2. Meaningful Discoveries, Proofs, and Empirical Dead Ends

### 2.1 Asymmetric Factor Leakage Theorem and `weft-slab/v0.1` (`verified`)

For a null-space adapter `Delta = B A` compiled so `A K0 = 0` ([05-spliceware-algebra-and-convergences.md](https://github.com/siliconcordat/loom/blob/main/docs/05-spliceware-algebra-and-convergences.md)), separate quantization yields `Q(B) Q(A) K0 = Q(B) (E_A K0)`. Rounding error `E_B` cancels to first order, while `E_A` drives base leakage.

- **Real `TinyStories-1M` confirmation ([weft E4](https://github.com/siliconcordat/weft/blob/main/experiments/e4_asymmetric_quant_packing.py), `N = 3` rank-4 adapters):** On `mlp.c_proj` (`64 x 256`), `int4/int8` cuts `K0` leakage `18.37x` vs `int4/int4` (`0.0293` vs `0.5391`) and `18.18x` vs reversed `int8/int4` (`0.5334`), keeping `15/16` top-1 predictions vs `6/16`.
- **Wide-output projections (`mlp.c_fc`, `256 x 64`):** `int4/int8` cuts `K0` leakage `20.03x` vs `int4/int4` while reducing bytes `39.0%` vs `int8/int8` and `68.8%` vs `f16/f16`. Asymmetric `int4/f16` beats uniform `bf16/bf16` on every metric (`7.4x` to `12.4x` lower leak in fewer bytes).
- **Latent Lattice FiLM confirmation (`weft` E7 and [07-latent-lattice-convergence.md](https://github.com/siliconcordat/loom/blob/main/docs/07-latent-lattice-convergence.md)):** `int4/int8` cuts conditioning leakage `14.42x` vs `int4/int4` on shared-carrier 4-layer E7 (`34.8%` smaller than `int8/int8`, `65.9%` vs `f16/f16`) and `22.60x` across `N = 6` FiLM adapters in `verify_lattice.py`.
- **`weft-slab/v0.1` binary container ([weft E5](https://github.com/siliconcordat/weft/blob/main/experiments/e5_noise_k0_scaling_and_readout.py) Part C):** Embeds `FixedRadixCertificate`, `base_weights_sha256`, L5 `zone_manifest_id`, canonical duplicate-key-rejected JSON header, and 32-byte SHA-256 trailer while cutting 4B `W_up` (`10240 x 2560`, `r = 16`) size `69.8%` vs F16 GGUF LoRA (`123,933` vs `410,016` bytes).
- **Scale boundary on loud columns:** `TinyStories-1M` `mlp.c_proj` column L2 norms are flat (`1.21` to `1.41` max-to-mean), so leaving 10 loud columns whole under INT4 lowers Layer-7 drift only slightly (`4.1657 -> 4.0039`). Outlier loud-column gains require 1.5B to 8B testing.

### 2.2 Live `llama-server 0.5.0` Superposition and Two Fail-Open Traps (`verified`)

In [weft E6](https://github.com/siliconcordat/weft/blob/main/experiments/e6_live_llama_server_lora.py) (`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` inside `llama-server 0.5.0`, build `11146`), two orthogonal rank-4 F16 GGUF v3 LoRAs plus an algebraic inverse `(-B0, A0)` ran on `blk.27.ffn_up.weight`:

- **Simultaneous composition and cancellation:** Active `[1.0, 1.0, 0.0]` shifted token-0 logprob by `-0.036992` (distinct from solo shifts `-0.005917` and `-0.019961`), while `[1.0, 0.0, 1.0]` cancelled back to base `W0` within `1.2e-7` max 16-token logprob diff. Zeroing scales plus `POST /slots/0?action=erase` restored bit-exact `W0` (`0.0` diff) in `0.99 ms`.
- **Two real upstream fail-open catches:** Passing repeated `--lora` flags alongside `--lora-init-without-apply` still initializes adapters at `scale = 1.0`. Fail-closed zero-init requires comma-separated `--lora-scaled <path>:0.0,...`. In addition, `POST /slots/{id}?action=erase` returns HTTP `501` unless `--slot-save-path` is passed at startup. Both traps are now guarded in `weft` and `warp`.

### 2.3 Seeded-Noise `K0` Rank Ceiling and Hybrid Cure (`verified`)

Negative findings in [weft E1](https://github.com/siliconcordat/weft/blob/main/experiments/e1_shared_token_composition.py) and E5 clarify how null-space calibration behaves without private text corpora:

- **Marchenko-Pastur `K0` calibration boundary (`weft` E1):** Undersampling `K0` at `n / d = 1` creates spurious tail null directions that cut train leakage `16x` while making unseen holdout leakage `4.3x` worse (`2.023 -> 8.630`). Oversampling (`n / d = 8`) reverses this (`12.8x` lower unseen leak, `100%` top-1 retention). Hard eigenspace clipping collapses usable dimension `m(t)` to `1` (`0.0%` pass) on full-rank SwiGLU activations, whereas soft covariance keeps `100%` pass.
- **Seeded-noise rank plateau (`weft` E5 Part A, `TinyStories-1M`):** Scaling uniform random vocabulary token noise from `n = 32` to `n = 2048` (zero real text read, 0-byte seed re-derivation) saturates at effective rank `2.42` vs `4.04` for real stories, plateauing at `52%` to `55%` (`53.2%` at `n = 2048`) unseen leakage recovery.
- **16-anchor hybrid cure:** Adding `16` public register-scoped `y = 0` anchors to seeded noise pushes unseen leakage recovery to `111.8%` (`0.218` vs `0.287` for real-text-only, max logit drift `7.78` vs `10.99`) at a lower target margin (`+4.49` vs `+9.81`).

### 2.4 Parametric Wire-Dialect Readout Bridge (`verified`)

The [tokenpage wire-readout study](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/tokenpage/pull/5) showed that `Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B` hidden states linearly separate compact wire glyphs while unadapted token readout drops to `0.558` (vs `1.000` JSON). `weft` E5 Part B reproduced this gap on real `TinyStories-1M` weights: a Layer-7 linear probe scored `1.000` (`16/16`) across four wire tags while unadapted readout scored `0.125` (`2/16`, margin `-2.3031`).

Compiling one rank-4 `int4/int8` hybrid readout adapter lifted unseen wire-tag accuracy from `0.125` to `1.000` (`16/16`, margin `+9.49`, `0.0` rollback diff) while cutting unseen narrative leakage `5.95x` vs naive adaptation (`0.839 -> 0.141`) and raising story top-1 retention from `1/16` to `13/16`.

### 2.5 Latent Lattice Convergence (`arXiv:2611.21608v1`, `verified` Analytical + 4-Layer CPU)

Analyzing Latent Lattice Language Models ([arXiv:2611.21608v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21608), Duggal et al., 16 Nov 2026, [project page](https://shivamduggal4.github.io/latent-lattice-language-models/)) across `loom` `docs/07`, `weft` E7, and `warp` yielded one capability unlock and three failure modes:

- **Zone-masked `L_0` cartridges beat shared-token interference:** Partitioning horizontal state into capability banks masked by L5 `zone-manifest/v0.1` collapses static vertical shared-token error from `8.877` to `1.15e-31` (`loom`). A 36-layer `d_r = 256` `L_0` cartridge occupies `18.0 KiB` (BF16), `640x` smaller than a 4B rank-16 F16 LoRA.
- **4-layer `weft` E7 verification:** Unrouted horizontal cartridges collapse `K0` (`5/24`, mixed formal `-0.5272`). Zone-routed `L_0` cartridges hit `24/24` `0.0` drift (`+8.4463` mixed formal, 0-byte re-derivation), and coupled vertical plus horizontal composition reaches `+9.0875` (`100%` pass).
- **Global `RMSNorm` cross-channel bleed:** Appending an orthogonal capability channel under shared `RMSNorm` rescales the untouched base slice by `24.77%` (`loom`). Per-bank `RMSNorm` restores exact `0.0` bleed.
- **`L_0` forgetting-gate decay horizon:** Injecting `L_0` into an unmodified base channel at `g = 0.95` retains only `0.989%` by token `t = 90`. Locking side-channel gates to `g_m = 1.0` inside active zone `z_m` preserves `100%` until clean zeroing on zone exit.
- **Persistent Horizontal-to-KV-Write Poisoning Law:** Lattice FiLM writes modulated keys and values into the KV cache, so flushing only KV pages after revoking a poisoned adapter fails. Surviving horizontal state re-poisons new KV writes (`30.260` KV-write drift and `8.130` logit drift in `weft` E7, `5.986` key drift in `loom`). Atomic multi-bank restore (`kv_cache`, `ssm_recurrent`, `lattice_horizontal_streams`) restores `0.0` drift in `warp` with `ERR_LATTICE_FLUSH_FAILED` quarantine.
- **L1 SRAM roofline vs dense-FiLM tax:** One FP32 horizontal snapshot uses `64.0 KiB` (`0.187 us` DMA) at 4B (`48.0x` smaller than 4B SSM) and `240.0 KiB` (`0.768 us`) at 27B (`51.2x` smaller), fitting 8 tagged checkpoints in on-chip SRAM (`512 KiB` to `1.875 MiB`). Naive dense FiLM on a 4B backbone would add `532.41M` params (`+13.28%` decode stall), confirming MDE FiLM must stay low-rank and asymmetrically packed.
